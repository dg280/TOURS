# 🚀 Growth Strategy: Visibility and Acquisition

## Tours & Detours Barcelona

This strategy turns the site from a simple storefront into a **booking engine** by reaching visitors at each stage of their decision-making process.

---

### 1. Local and Long-Tail SEO (Organic Acquisition)

Aim to rank for niche searches with less competition than "Hotel Barcelona".

- **Target intent keywords**: Optimize each tour page for specific searches:
  - _Primary_: "Private Medieval villages tour from Barcelona".
  - _Additional_: "Private Montserrat tour without crowds", "Hidden Costa Brava tour with an English-speaking guide".
- **Google Business Profile (critical)**:
  - Create or optimize the Google Maps listing.
  - Systematically request a review after every tour by sending customers the direct link.
  - Publish a weekly Google Post featuring recent tour photos.

### 2. Social Proof Strategy (Building Trust)

In tourism, people buy trust before they buy an experience.

- **Instagram "Living Story"**:
  - Do not only post attractive photos. Post **videos of yourself speaking** and explaining anecdotes. You are the product.
  - Use Highlights for each tour with customer feedback.
- **Micro-influencer partnerships**:
  - Identify French- and English-language travel Instagram or TikTok accounts with 5,000 to 20,000 followers.
  - Offer a complimentary tour in exchange for three stories and one post. This is currently the most cost-effective acquisition channel.

### 3. The Hook (Audience Capture)

Ninety percent of visitors do not book on their first visit, so retain a way to contact them.

- **Email lead magnet**:
  - Do not only ask visitors to subscribe to the newsletter.
  - Offer: _"Download my free PDF guide: 10 secret tapas spots tourists do not know about."_
  - Use the email list collected in the admin interface to send a special Early Bird or Last Minute offer.

### 4. Distribution Networks (Leverage)

Use the reach of large marketplaces while protecting the direct channel.

- **80/20 strategy**:
  - List tours on **Viator** and **GetYourGuide** for visibility; they spend heavily on advertising.
  - On the direct Vercel site, offer a **slightly lower price** or a small **exclusive extra**, such as a complimentary coffee or professional souvenir photo.
  - State on social channels: _"The best price is always direct on our website."_

### 5. Technology and Monitoring

- **Load speed**: The current Vercel site is already very fast, a major advantage for mobile SEO.
- **Conversion**: Ensure the Book button is visible without scrolling on mobile.

---

### Monthly Acquisition Checklist

1. [ ] Post two behind-the-scenes Instagram Reels each week.
2. [ ] Send a helpful, non-aggressive sales email to newsletter subscribers.
3. [ ] Reply to every Google review, including positive ones, using keywords such as _"We are delighted you enjoyed this **private Montserrat tour**..."_.
4. [ ] Check Supabase latency in the admin dashboard.

**The goal is for direct, commission-free website bookings to represent 50% of all bookings within six months.**
